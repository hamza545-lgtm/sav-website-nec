Frequently asked questions

How does Savnec source its experts?+

We don't draw from a pre-built roster. Every engagement starts with fresh, targeted sourcing through professional networks and direct outreach, matched to the specific project at hand.

How is compliance handled?+

Every expert is screened for conflicts of interest, competing NDAs, and non-competes before being matched to a client, and re-screened for each new engagement. See our Compliance section for full detail.

How quickly can I be matched with an expert?+

Timelines depend on the scope and specificity of the request. Submit a request through our Clients section and our team will follow up in less than 24 hours with next steps.

Is my information kept confidential?+

Yes. Client identities, project scopes, and expert details are treated as confidential and are only shared as necessary to facilitate an engagement.

How are experts compensated?+

Experts are paid a transparent rate per completed engagement, disclosed before they accept a request. See our Experts section for more.

Who can I contact with a compliance or legal question?+

Reach our team directly at compliance@savnec.com.
