Compliance & data security

Trust is the foundation of every engagement on Savnec. Our compliance program is designed to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and legal safety of every client and expert interaction.

Our commitment to compliance

Savnec operates under a strict internal compliance framework aligned with recognized market research industry standards, including the ESOMAR/ICC International Code on market and social research. Every consultation, survey, and expert engagement conducted through Savnec is subject to these standards.

Conflict of interest screening

Before any expert is matched to a client engagement, Savnec screens for existing employment relationships, competing NDAs, non-compete restrictions, and board or advisory positions that could create a conflict. Experts are required to disclose any potential conflicts prior to acceptance of an engagement, and disclosed conflicts are reviewed before a match is confirmed.

Confidentiality & non-disclosure

All clients and experts engaging through Savnec operate under confidentiality terms. Client identities, project scopes, and the substance of expert consultations are treated as confidential and are not disclosed beyond what is necessary to facilitate the engagement.

Prohibition on material non-public information (MNPI)

Savnec strictly prohibits the solicitation, discussion, or exchange of material non-public information in any consultation. Experts are instructed that engagements are limited to general industry knowledge, publicly available information, and professional experience — never confidential or non-public information about a current or former employer.

Data security

Client and expert data submitted to Savnec is stored securely and access is limited to authorized personnel only. Savnec does not sell or share personal or business data with third parties outside the scope of facilitating an engagement.

Industry standards

Savnec's compliance practices are informed by ESOMAR/ICC guidelines for market research ethics, reflecting the standards applied across the expert network industry.

Questions
