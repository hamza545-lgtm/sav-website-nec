1. Overview

This Privacy & Cookie Policy ("Policy") explains how Savnec LLC ("Savnec," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards personal information in connection with our website and services, including our expert network, research consultations, and related offerings (together, the "Services"). This Policy applies to clients, prospective clients, experts and expert applicants ("Experts"), and visitors to our website.

By using our Services or submitting information through our website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy. If you do not agree with this Policy, please do not use our Services.

2. Information we collect

We collect information in the following ways:

Information you provide directly. This includes your name, business email address, phone number, company name and type, region, job title, industry or area of expertise, LinkedIn profile, and any details you include in a message, request, or application submitted through our forms.

Information collected automatically. When you visit our website, we may automatically collect certain technical information such as your browser type, device type, IP address, pages visited, and how you interact with our site, generally through cookies and similar technologies described in Section 5.

Information from third parties. Where relevant to onboarding or vetting an Expert, we may collect professional background information from publicly available sources, such as professional networking platforms, to verify experience and screen for conflicts of interest.

3. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

Respond to inquiries and administer client and Expert relationships; match client research requests with relevant, vetted Experts; conduct conflict-of-interest and compliance screening; process Expert applications and engagements, including compensation; operate, maintain, and improve our website and Services; communicate with you about your request, application, or account; and comply with applicable legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations.

We do not use personal information for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects without human involvement.

4. How we share your information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information in the following limited circumstances:

With clients and Experts, as necessary to facilitate a specific engagement (for example, sharing an Expert's relevant background with a client, or a client's project scope with a matched Expert).

With service providers, such as email delivery, form processing, and hosting providers, who process information on our behalf and are bound by confidentiality obligations.

For legal reasons, where disclosure is required to comply with applicable law, regulation, legal process, or a valid governmental request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Savnec, our clients, our Experts, or others.

In a business transaction, such as a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, in which personal information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to standard confidentiality protections.

5. Cookies & similar technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, understand how visitors use our site, and improve functionality over time. Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser.

We use these technologies for essential site functionality (such as remembering form inputs during a session) and for basic, aggregated analytics to understand site usage. We do not currently use third-party advertising cookies.

Most browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings, including blocking or deleting them. Doing so may affect the functionality of certain parts of our website. To learn more about managing cookies, consult your browser's help documentation.

6. Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, including to maintain client and Expert relationships, comply with legal and regulatory obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When information is no longer needed for these purposes, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it.

7. Data security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. Access to personal information is limited to personnel who need it to perform their role. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International data transfers

Savnec is headquartered in the United States and serves clients and Experts globally. If you are located outside the United States, your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States or other countries where we or our service providers operate. Where required by applicable law, we take steps intended to ensure that such transfers are subject to appropriate safeguards.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, which may include the right to: access the personal information we hold about you; request correction of inaccurate information; request deletion of your information; object to or restrict certain processing; and, where applicable (including for residents of the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and certain U.S. states), request that your information be transferred in a portable format.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in Section 14. We will respond to verified requests within a reasonable timeframe and in accordance with applicable law. If you are an Expert, you may also request to opt out of further participation in the network at any time by contacting us directly.

10. Children's privacy

Our Services are intended for business use by professionals and are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

11. Marketing preferences

We may occasionally send communications related to our Services, including updates relevant to a request or application you have submitted. Where we send promotional communications, you may opt out at any time by following the unsubscribe instructions included in the message or by contacting us directly. Certain non-promotional communications, such as those necessary to administer an active engagement, are not subject to opt-out.

12. Third-party links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, including LinkedIn and other professional networking platforms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these third-party sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any third-party site you visit.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when this Policy was most recently revised. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

14. Contact us

If you have questions about this Policy or how your information is handled, please contact us at compliance@savnec.com, or through our Contact section.
