How we recruit and vet experts

Every expert on Savnec goes through the same process before they're eligible to be matched to a client. We don't pull from a static database ‐ each engagement starts with fresh, targeted sourcing built around your specific need.

01

Sourcing

Targeted outreach across professional networks, referrals, and direct sourcing to identify candidates with genuine, current expertise.

02

Credential verification

Current or recent role, tenure, and relevant experience are confirmed before an expert is added to the network.

03

Conflict screening

Every candidate is checked against existing NDAs, non-competes, and board or advisory positions before activation.

04

Ongoing re-verification

Activated experts are re-screened for new conflicts each time they're proposed for an engagement ‐ not just once at sign-up.
